Page rules & scam safetyHow this page works, what we post, and how to rent safely in Dublin.

How this page works

  • Free, always. We never ask for money, deposits or bank details. Anyone who does isn't us.
  • Three forms, link in bio: find housemates, list a room or sublet, or ask for a place.
  • Contact happens by Instagram DM with the handle on each post. We never publish emails or phone numbers.

Page rules

  • No discriminatory requirements. Nothing that excludes people by gender, civil or family status, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, race, membership of the Traveller community, or getting HAP / housing assistance.
  • Lifestyle preferences are fine: non-smoker, tidy, quiet, pets or no pets, working hours.
  • Sublets need written landlord consent if you're a tenant. No consent, no post.
  • Be respectful in DMs. Harassment or pressure gets you blocked and your posts removed.
  • Your data: we delete form answers 90 days after you submit, or sooner if you ask.

Scam red flags 🚩

  • Deposit or rent before a viewing "to hold the room" or "lots of interest".
  • "I'm abroad, I'll post you the keys."
  • Payment by Western Union, MoneyGram, crypto, gift cards or Revolut to a stranger.
  • Moving the chat to WhatsApp, Telegram or email straight away.
  • Too cheap for the area plus pressure to decide today.
  • Won't show ID or the lease, or keeps postponing the viewing.

Seen one of these on a post here? DM us and we'll take it down.

Before you pay anything

  • View it in person or by live video call with the person who'll hand you the keys.
  • See ID and proof of tenancy, plus the landlord's written consent if it's a sublet.
  • Get it in writing: rent, deposit, dates and bills, before money moves.
  • Pay by bank transfer, not cash, and keep the receipt.
  • Renting from a landlord? Check the tenancy is registered with the RTB (rtb.ie).

If something's off

  • Tell us: DM @dublin_rentshare with the post and what happened. We pull posts first and check after.
  • Lost money? Report it to your local Garda station and your bank as soon as you can.
  • Facing losing your home? Threshold, the national housing charity, can help: threshold.ie

Know your rightsChecked Oct 2026Renting in Dublin: your rights, free advice, helplines and where to get support. Save this.

Your rights as a renter

  • Rent increases are capped. Tenancies that started on or after 1 March 2026 can only go up by inflation (CPI) or 2%, whichever is lower. Check yours on rtb.ie.
  • Tenancies must be registered with the RTB, which also handles deposit and rent disputes.
  • Refusing HAP is discrimination. If you're refused because you get HAP or other housing support, send the landlord an ES1 form within 2 months, then complain to the WRC within 6 months.
  • Renting a room in the owner's home? You're usually a licensee, not a tenant, so most RTB protections don't apply. Get the terms in writing.

Information, not legal advice. Checked Oct 2026.

Free advice

  • Threshold: notices to quit, deposits, repairs, rent. Freephone 1800 454 454 · threshold.ie
  • Residential Tenancies Board: disputes, deposits, the rent register. rtb.ie
  • Citizens Information: 0818 07 4000 · citizensinformation.ie
  • FLAC: free basic legal information. 01 906 1010 · flac.ie

Got a notice of termination? Call Threshold early, before you move out.

If you're losing your home

  • Homeless Freephone, Dublin: 1800 707 707, 10am–10pm every day, for emergency accommodation.
  • Threshold: 1800 454 454, for help to keep your tenancy if you're at risk of homelessness.
  • Sleeping rough tonight? Dublin Street Outreach: 01 872 0185.
  • In danger right now? Call 999 or 112.

Information, not legal advice. Checked Oct 2026.

You're not on your own

  • Join a tenants' union. The Community Action Tenants Union (CATU) has branches across Dublin, where renters organise together on rent hikes, evictions and bad conditions. catuireland.org
  • Talk to your neighbours. The problems you have with your landlord are usually shared.
  • Keep records: your lease, receipts, messages, photos of the place and any problems, with dates.
